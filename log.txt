Fit anatomy (paper notes; details in the central README.md):
  HERA input: seven combined H1+ZEUS NC/CC inclusive-DIS cards from arXiv:1506.06042; Q2 > 7.5 GeV2 leaves 1056 points.
  CMS input: 2016 anti-kT R=0.7 inclusive jets from arXiv:2111.10431 in four |y| bins; 78 active points plus ten statistical-correlation blocks.
  Jet theory: fastNLO 2.5.0 convolves four 1jet.NNLO.fnl5332h_y{0,1,2,3}_ptjet.tab grids at muR=muF=pT.
  Jet corrections: each rapidity prediction is F*NP*EW using NP/NP_y*.dat and EW/EW_y*.dat binwise K factors.
  DIS theory: QCDNUM evolves the PDFs/alpha_s at NNLO; RT_DISNC handles NC reduced cross sections and BaseDISCC handles CC.
  Fit engine: xFitter builds 1134 predictions and the nuisance/covariance chi2; MINUIT strategy 2 varies 15 PDF-shape parameters plus alpha_s and then runs HESSE.
  Numerics/output: OpenBLAS/LAPACK handle matrix algebra; ROOT and xfitter-draw are available for inspecting and plotting the resulting PDF/error files.
  Reproducibility caveat: this build uses QCDNUM 18-00/00 whereas the publication reports 17-01/14, so numerical agreement must be validated.
